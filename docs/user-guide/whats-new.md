# What's new card

At the top of most workspace pages, a short **What's new** card lists
what changed for you since you last looked. Each line opens the page
(or the exact event or conversation) it's about. On a quiet day the
card doesn't show at all; Home says "You're all caught up."

## Got it and Later

- **Got it** marks everything on the card as seen. The card stays away
  until something new happens. It remembers this on your account, so
  it follows you to another computer or your phone.
- **Later** hides the card for the rest of this browser session
  without marking anything seen. Open a new browser session and it's
  back.
- **Reminders** (a pay week closing, a required event tomorrow) are
  about a date, not a change. Got it silences that one reminder — for
  that week or that event — and the next one still shows.

Click a line to see the items behind it; click an item to go there.
Lines are ordered by urgency: red needs you now, yellow is waiting for
your review, blue is a reminder, gray is good to know.

## What you'll see

**Interns** — timesheets that came back or got a coordinator comment,
new tasks for you, feedback on your work, "submit your hours" near the
end of a pay week (if you have drafts or are under your expected
weekly hours), required events today or tomorrow, hours approved for
payroll, new events, and new messages.

**Partner organizations** — tasks marked Need Help, work ready for your
review, new posts on events your organization hosts, new events, and
new messages. Timesheets never appear here.

**Coordinators** — tasks marked Need Help, messages flagged for a
supervisor, timesheets to review, intern replies to staff comments,
required events with no attendance taken, interns under their expected
hours near the end of a pay week, work that has waited on a partner's
review for 3+ days, **employer activity** (tasks employers requested
and events they scheduled), and new messages in conversations you're
part of.

**Admins** — everything coordinators see, plus pay weeks ready to
finalize (with the auto-approve date) and weeks that were finalized.

## Mark reviewed from the card (coordinators and admins)

Open **timesheets to review**, tick the entries you've looked at, and
choose **Mark reviewed**, then confirm. This is the same as Mark
reviewed on the Review timesheets page: it records the coordinator
review only — payroll approval is still the admin's step. To leave a
comment instead, use **Open Review timesheets**.

## Pages without the card

Reports, the user guides, Who am I, and Stopwatch skip the card so
printouts and small widgets stay clean.
