# Messages & Inbox

One inbox for every conversation in the workspace — questions on
tasks, notes on your hours, event discussions, and direct messages.

## The inbox

Threads sort by latest activity; a red dot means something new since
you last opened the inbox. Click a thread to read it and reply.

Use the **All / Received / Sent** tabs to narrow the list — **Sent**
shows the conversations you started.

**New message** starts a direct or group conversation — pick any
people on the program (your mentor, a teammate, the coordinator).

## Where threads come from

- **Task threads** appear when work is submitted, accepted, or sent
  back with a note — everyone connected to the task sees them.
- **Hours threads** appear when the coordinator returns an entry for
  clarification — visible to you and program staff only, never to
  your employer.
- **Direct messages** are visible to the people in them.

## Who can see what

Every thread has an audience, and the system enforces it — you only
ever see threads meant for you. One rule applies to everything:
**program staff can read all conversations**, including direct
messages. This is program infrastructure, like a work email account —
supportive supervision, not surveillance, and the composer reminds you
of it.

## The home page

Your workspace home shows what needs attention — new messages, open
and overdue tasks, hours logged this week, anything returned to you —
plus quick buttons for logging hours, updating tasks, and asking for
help. Click any card to jump to the right place.
