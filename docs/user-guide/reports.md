# Reports

## Weekly intern report (coordinator & admin)

Open **Reports** and pick a week. You get one row per intern:

- **Hours submitted / approved** for that week.
- **Tasks completed** that week, plus **tasks open** and **overdue**.
- **Required events attended / missed** — events marked *Required*
  for the intern's cohort that week.

Overdue tasks and missed required events show in red so they stand out.
A **Total** row sums the numbers across interns.

To share with program supervisors: **Export CSV** or **Export XLSX**
for a spreadsheet, or **Print / PDF** for a clean one-page handout
(the buttons and week picker are hidden in the printout).
